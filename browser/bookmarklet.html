The AskMaddi Bookmarklet

From any page to the card that answers it — one click.

Ask Maddi

Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar. (Clicking it here does nothing — it only works once it lives in your bookmarks.)

What it does

You're reading a thread where someone asks whether the Sony 100-400 is worth it, or a review that name-drops five lenses. Highlight the product name, click Ask Maddi, and a new tab opens on the AskMaddi card — the whole consensus across every review we've read, in one place. No selection? It uses the page title.

How to install

  1. Make your bookmarks bar visible (Ctrl/⌘+Shift+B in most browsers).
  2. Drag the orange Ask Maddi button above onto it.
  3. On any page, select a product name and click your new bookmark.

Why bother

A Maddi card is built to be shared — it carries a ready-to-paste summary with an honest “I built this” disclosure. The bookmarklet gets you to the card; the card's Share buttons get you the paste. Answer the question where it was asked.
