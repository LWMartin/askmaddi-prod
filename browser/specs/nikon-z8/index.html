Nikon z8 — spec questions and answers

Factual answers to common spec questions about the Nikon z8, drawn only from its published specifications.

What is the native ISO range of the Nikon z8?

Native range of ISO 64-25,600 (expandable to 32-102,400)

How much does the Nikon z8 weigh?

910g (with battery)

What is the EVF resolution of the Nikon z8?

Quad-VGA (1280x960) EVF (3690000 dots), 0.8x magnification with 50 mm lens, 3000 nits brightness

What battery does the Nikon z8 use?

EN-EL15c (backwards compatible with EN-EL15a/b batteries); USB-PD rechargeable
