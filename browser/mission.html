Against the flood.

Why we built AskMaddi differently.

"Morality, beauty, and truth over revenue or scale."

The Problem

Shopping for gear online means wading through a flood. The same camera body listed twenty different ways. The wrong variant surfaced first — an A7R IV when you searched the A7 IV. Accessories and knock-offs dressed up to look like the real thing. Every result tuned to sell you something, not to answer your question.

That flood isn't a bug. It's the store model working exactly as intended — more listings, more upsell, more time on page. We think the opposite is worth building.

Our Approach

AskMaddi is built on a simple principle: help you find the exact thing, then get out of the way.

Every listing is collapsed to the real product. We hold a strict line on identity — an A7 IV is not an A7R IV, a 24-70 is not a 24-105 — so a search returns the thing you asked for, not its near-cousins. Where we've done the homework, the product carries a neutral breakdown synthesized from dozens of real reviews: what owners agree on, what they don't, and what they complain about after six months. We present fit, not a verdict — never a "best," just the evidence and who it suits.

One honest front door — search — over a deepening floor of researched cards. No flood, no wrong variant, no sales pitch.

Our Values

  • Precision over volume — the exact product beats a hundred almost-right ones. We'd rather show you five real matches than a wall of noise.
  • Neutral by default — we present fit, not ratings. No "best," no hype, no manufactured urgency. If reviewers flag a weakness, it stays on the card.
  • No pay-to-rank, ever — nothing ranks higher because someone paid for it, and no ads follow you around. Your attention is not for sale.
  • A human signs every card — machines do the reading and sorting; a person makes the final call before anything ships.

How We Sustain This

When you find a product through AskMaddi and choose to buy it, we may earn a small affiliate commission from the retailer. It costs you nothing extra, we disclose it on every page, and — this is the part that matters — the commission is the same either way, so it never shapes what a card says or how a result ranks.

That model aligns our interests with yours: we succeed when we help you find what you're actually looking for. We are not private search — our affiliate relationships mean a query does touch a retailer's systems — and we'd rather say so plainly than dress up a promise we can't keep. What we can promise is precision and neutrality, and both are demonstrable in the first five seconds you use it.

AskMaddi is part of a larger vision — building technology that serves the person using it rather than the other way around. The best tools respect your time and your judgment.

Thank you for being here. Every search you run through AskMaddi is a vote for a different kind of internet.
